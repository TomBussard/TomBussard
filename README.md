# Tom Bussard

I am a counterparty risk analyst apprentice at BNP Paribas CIB and a master's student in Economics and Financial Engineering at Université Paris Dauphine PSL. My recent work focuses on derivatives pricing, volatility and the risks that become visible when you move from a single instrument to a portfolio.
